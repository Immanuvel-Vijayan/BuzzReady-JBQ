❓ How to play

Tap the card or press Space to reveal the next part. Once the full question shows you have 25 seconds to answer and mark Pass or Fail (Pass +1 / +2 / +3, Fail −1 / −2 / −3 for 10 / 20 / 30-pointers). If time runs out the card counts as failed. Skip moves a card to the bottom with 0 points. Pause stops the clock until you resume; Pass or Fail starts a fresh countdown on the next card. After the answer, mark it Pass or Fail. A failed card goes back to the bottom of the pile and comes round again.
